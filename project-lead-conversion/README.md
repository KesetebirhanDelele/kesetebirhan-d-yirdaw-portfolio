# Lead Conversion

## Project Overview

Automates the conversion of cold leads into active participants in an AI class through engagement tracking.

---

## Business Problem

This application addresses the challenge of converting cold leads into active participants in an AI class, specifically targeting individuals who have shown initial interest but have not engaged further. By automating interactions and tracking engagement, it helps organizations effectively nurture these leads.

---

## Objective

- Automate the invitation process for cold leads to a free AI class.
- Track engagement signals and course progress to identify active participants.
- Facilitate seamless handoff of 'hot leads' to the booking system.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- FastAPI
- Pydantic
- Uvicorn
- Pytest
- OpenAI

---

## Project Workflow

- Cold leads are invited to participate in a free AI class through the AI agent, Cora.
- Engagement signals are tracked as leads interact with the course material.
- The system computes course progress and identifies 'hot leads' based on engagement.
- Hot leads are prepared for handoff to the booking system via GoHighLevel.

---

## Key Insights

- The architecture effectively manages complexity across 10 interconnected components, ensuring a smooth workflow.
- By employing deterministic scripts, the system guarantees reliable execution of business logic without orchestration concerns.
- The use of AI agents for planning and validation enhances code quality and reduces errors in the development process.
