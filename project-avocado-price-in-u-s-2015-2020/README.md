# Avocado Price in U.S. (2015-2020)

## Project Overview

Analyzes and visualizes avocado pricing data to help users understand market trends.

---

## Business Problem

Consumers are increasingly concerned about the rising prices of avocados, a popular health food. This project addresses the need for understanding the price trends and volume of avocado sales across different states in the U.S. from 2015 to 2020.

---

## Objective

- Analyze average avocado prices and volumes in various U.S. states.
- Identify factors influencing price fluctuations, including type and seasonality.
- Provide insights to consumers and stakeholders about avocado market trends.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Department of Agriculture data
- CityState table
- KPI visualizations
- Data quality assessment tools

---

## Project Workflow

- Gather data on avocado prices and volumes from 2015 to 2020.
- Analyze the impact of avocado type (organic vs. conventional) on pricing.
- Create visualizations to represent data trends and insights.
- Deploy the final dashboard using Power BI to share findings.

---

## Key Insights

- The average national price of avocado rose from $1.17 in July 2018 to $2.10 in July 2019.
- Organic avocados have a higher average price but lower volume compared to conventional avocados.
- The COVID-19 pandemic significantly affected avocado prices and volumes in 2019.
