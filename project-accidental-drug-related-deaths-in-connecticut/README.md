# Accidental Drug Related Deaths In Connecticut

## Project Overview

Visualizes drug overdose statistics and trends to inform viewers about the severity of the crisis.

---

## Business Problem

The project addresses the growing crisis of drug overdoses in Connecticut, highlighting the alarming increase in accidental drug-related deaths over a six-year period. It aims to raise awareness about the severity and rapid evolution of this public health issue.

---

## Objective

- Present data on accidental drug-related deaths in Connecticut from 2012-2018.
- Visualize key performance indicators (KPIs) and insights to provide context to the data.
- Identify year-over-year statistics and potential future trends related to drug overdoses.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Data visualization dashboard
- KPI metrics
- Statistical analysis
- Trend analysis
- Year-over-year comparison

---

## Project Workflow

- Collect data on accidental drug-related deaths in Connecticut from 2012-2018.
- Analyze the data to identify key trends and statistics.
- Create a dashboard to visualize the findings and KPIs.
- Highlight significant insights and age-related statistics.
- Present the data clearly to convey the urgency of the epidemic.

---

## Key Insights

- The project covers a six-year period from 2012 to 2018, showcasing the evolution of drug-related deaths.
- Year-over-year statistics reveal significant trends in accidental drug overdoses.
- Age-related statistics provide context to the demographics most affected by the crisis.
