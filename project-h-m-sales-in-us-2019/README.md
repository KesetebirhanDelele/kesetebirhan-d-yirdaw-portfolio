# H&M Sales in US 2019

## Project Overview

Visualizes and analyzes H&M's sales data from 2019, offering insights into sales performance across categories.

---

## Business Problem

H&M faced challenges in understanding their sales performance across the US in 2019, particularly in the context of fluctuating consumer demand and competition in the fast fashion industry. The project aimed to provide insights into sales trends and profitability to inform strategic decisions.

---

## Objective

- Analyze monthly sales performance by category and region.
- Visualize key performance indicators such as month-over-month sales and gross profit margin.
- Identify actionable insights to enhance sales strategies and customer engagement.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Data visualization techniques
- Sales data analysis
- KPI tracking
- Dashboard creation

---

## Project Workflow

- Gathered sales data for H&M across the US for the year 2019.
- Defined key performance indicators including sales per month, MOM%, and GPM.
- Developed a dashboard to visualize sales trends by category, state, and region.
- Analyzed insights from the dashboard to identify both positive and negative sales trends.
- Provided recommendations for improving sales strategies based on the analysis.

---

## Key Insights

- Sales decreased by 47% in the 4th quarter with a 14% discount offered.
- Sales in the 3rd quarter increased by 164% when a 26% discount was applied.
- Customer retention improvements of just 5% can lead to a 25% increase in profit.
