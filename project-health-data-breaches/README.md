# Health Data Breaches

## Project Overview

Analyzes health data breaches to identify trends and develop preventive measures.

---

## Business Problem

Health data breaches pose significant risks to patient privacy and can lead to costly lawsuits for healthcare organizations. This project addresses the need to analyze and understand the common causes of these breaches to enhance cybersecurity measures.

---

## Objective

- Analyze health data breaches reported between 2019 and 2021.
- Identify common types of breaches and their causes.
- Develop strategies to prevent future data loss and associated legal issues.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- CSV files
- DAX
- Data visualization techniques
- US Department of Health and Human Services data

---

## Project Workflow

- Import health data breach records from a CSV file.
- Create a date table for better data analysis.
- Design and format the dashboard with relevant visuals.
- Group data by breach type and assess employee involvement.
- Evaluate findings to propose preventive measures.

---

## Key Insights

- The project focuses on health data breaches reported by the US Department of Health and Human Services from 2019 to 2021.
- Data is categorized by breach type, including hacking and improper disposal.
- Visuals indicate whether a business associate was present at the time of the breach.
