# Cosmetic Product Industry Insights

## Project Overview

This application provides valuable insights into the cosmetic product industry by analyzing and visualizing trends in product counts, chemical usage, and market dynamics over time. It is designed for industry professionals, marketers, and researchers seeking to make informed decisions based on data-driven trends.

A key design decision was to focus on user-friendly visualizations, allowing users to easily interpret complex data and identify significant trends without requiring extensive technical expertise.

---

## Business Problem

The cosmetic product industry in California faces significant challenges due to fluctuations in company count, chemical count, and product discontinuation rates. These variations can lead to decreased market competitiveness and necessitate strategic planning to adapt to changing market conditions.

---

## Objective

- Analyze product count and chemical usage trends from 2009 to 2020.
- Identify year-over-year fluctuations and their implications for the industry.
- Provide insights for strategic decision-making and portfolio diversification.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- Data visualization techniques
- Dashboard design
- Data cleaning techniques
- DAX formulas

---

## Project Workflow

- Gather and analyze data on cosmetic products from 2009 to 2020.
- Identify key performance indicators (KPIs) relevant to the industry.
- Create visual representations of data, including charts and word clouds.
- Develop an interactive dashboard to present insights.
- Iterate on the dashboard based on stakeholder feedback.

---

## Key Insights

- Some metrics show decreases of up to 71%, while others have increased by as much as 124%.
- Yearly trends indicate both significant growth and declines in the industry.
- The product category chart aids in understanding the distribution of products across categories.
